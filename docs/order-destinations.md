# Destination requirements and detail references

A station can declare the requirements of an input in `x-family.production.handoffs[].requires`. Each entry has a stable `id` and `es`/`en` instructions. `hub_station_catalog` exposes them in the handoff and in each matching `chain` step. The Hub supplies defaults for the volume, video and print handoffs. A manifest can override their wording by id. Existing Orders plans also recognise the usual mesh, video, figure and playable scene kinds for their corresponding stations.

Use `hub_agora_plan_set` to connect jobs with `after`. The order traces each requiring job back through its predecessors. In `hub_agora_order` and `hub_agora_job`, `requirements` lists the instructions with `from.task_id`, `from.station` and `from.title`. A requirement is shown once per source job even where paths meet. Removing a job or changing dependencies recalculates the requirements from the active plan, and the ordinary plan undo restores them. The Orders page shows each requirement and its source beside the affected job.

Attach a reference while checking a detail with `hub_agora_piece_check`. In a `keep` mark, use `anchor: {piece, preview?, view?, region?, palette?, measure?, sha256?}`. `piece` is the registered id of a piece in the same order. `preview` is the zero-based index of a saved preview. A region is `[x, y, width, height]` in fractions of the image, with the origin at the top left. A palette is a list of `#RRGGBB` values. A measurement has `value` and `unit`. An optional `sha256` verifies that the chosen bytes are the expected ones.

The Hub returns the registered revision, byte digest, selector digest (`anchor_sha256`) and a link to its bytes. A check on the sheet can pin the tube to the profile image, for example. Checks retain the anchor used and descendants inherit the anchor with the detail, pending their own check. The next worker also gets `anchored_details` from explicit inputs, kept references and upstream jobs. The piece cards link to the pinned reference. Use the check's undo to restore its previous detail and recorded checks.
